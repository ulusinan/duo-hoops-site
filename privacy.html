Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short: Duo Hoops does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (levels cleared, stars, best scores) and your settings (sound effects, music, haptics) on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full Game" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase is owned, so it can unlock the content and restore it on your other devices. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Device sensors

On iPhone Duo the game reads the hinge angle to draw the 3D court correctly. This reading is used live on the device and is never recorded or transmitted.

Children

Duo Hoops is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
