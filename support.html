Help with the game, purchases and iPhone Duo.

Contact: open a support request (free GitHub account needed)
We usually reply within two working days.

How do I shoot?

Put your finger on the ball on the court and flick it up toward the hoop. A smooth, straight flick at a medium speed scores most. Faster flicks go further, a sideways flick aims left or right.

How does it work on iPhone Duo?

Set the phone on a table and open it to about 90 degrees. The bottom half becomes the court and the top half shows the hoop in 3D. On other iPhones the game shows the same view on one screen.

What does the Full Game include?

Levels 1 and 2 are free. The one-time Full Game purchase unlocks all 15 levels, Endless mode and Around the World. There are no ads and no subscription.

I bought the Full Game on another device

Open any level marked with a crown and tap Restore. The purchase follows your Apple Account.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Sound, music and vibration

Tap the gear button in the menu (or Sound in the pause menu) to turn sound effects, music and haptics on or off. The ring/silent switch also mutes the game.
